The model
01
The problem

Frontier multimodal AI can already understand images and video. Bringing that intelligence continuously into physical environments requires sensing, compute, connectivity, memory, power, and reliable infrastructure. Africa is one of the clearest places to solve this first.

02
The first wedge

Cameras are the first eyes of Physical AI — the most general-purpose, affordable way to give AI visibility into physical environments. Where camera infrastructure exists, we make it intelligent. Where it doesn't, we deploy the sensing layer end to end.

03
The loop

Deploy useful intelligence. Learn from physical environments. Build better infrastructure. Advance the intelligence. Each layer earns the next — and with permission and privacy safeguards, every deployment compounds our physical-world intelligence.

04 First product

Gateman-AI

Conversational Physical AI for camera-enabled environments.

A small box on-site. Events, not footage. The interface is not surveillance software. It is conversation.

Gateman-AI turns camera streams into structured, time-aware intelligence. A small box on-site plugs into cameras — yours or ours — and instead of hours of footage nobody watches, you get events: it understands scenes, remembers what happened, answers questions, and proactively surfaces the activity that matters.

  • →Conversational, grounded. Ask in plain language and it answers from what it actually saw, never invented
  • →On-premise, resilient. The box keeps capturing through power and network cuts, buffering locally and syncing when the connection returns
  • →Events, not footage. Each event is text plus one key frame — ~38 KB for a day of activity, not gigabytes of video. Cheap to store, instant to search
  • →Standing watches. Tell it once — “alert me if anyone lingers more than two minutes” — and it watches, day and night
  • →WhatsApp-native. No new app to learn — alerts and answers arrive where you already are
